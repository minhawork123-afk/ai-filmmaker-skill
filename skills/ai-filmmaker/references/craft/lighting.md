# Craft — Lighting

Light is the main signal that separates photographed from generated. Unlabelled content is **[General best practice]**; workflow-proven rules are marked **[Tested]**.

## The rule [Tested]

Name the physical light source: what is producing the light, where it is, its colour and its quality. Never write "good lighting" or "cinematic lighting" without naming the source.

```text
WEAK    Dramatic lighting in the kitchen.
STRONG  A single warm under-cabinet strip as the key, soft falloff; cold blue pre-dawn light from a window just out of frame.
WHY     Two named sources with positions and colours give the model a real room to build.
```

## Describe every source with four properties

1. **Source**: a window, a tungsten lamp, an LED batten, a phone screen, a streetlight, firelight, the sun, an overcast sky.
2. **Position**: camera-LEFT or RIGHT, behind the subject, overhead, low; in frame (a practical) or just out of frame.
3. **Colour**: warm amber tungsten, neutral daylight, cold blue moonlight, sodium-vapour orange, magenta neon.
4. **Quality**: hard (sharp shadows, small source) or soft (large source, wrapped shadows); the falloff; the contrast.

## Setups and what they say

| Setup | Look | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Motivated practical key | a lamp, screen or window in the scene is the main light | realism in any genre [Tested pattern] |
| Mixed colour temperature | warm practical against a cold window, the two sources visibly disagreeing | lived-in interiors, dawn and dusk realism [Tested] |
| Hard directional window daylight | long shadow bars across the floor | gyms, studios, drama, "propped phone in good light" [Tested] |
| Overcast ambient only | flat, soft, low contrast | bleak exteriors, documentary, northern realism [Tested] |
| Golden hour sun, low and behind | rim light, flare, warm haze | romance, nostalgia, lifestyle |
| Three-point (key, fill, back) | clean, controlled | interviews, corporate, product |
| Rembrandt (45° key, triangle on the far cheek) | sculpted, classical | drama portraits |
| Split light (90° side) | half the face in shadow | duality, threat, noir |
| Top light | eye sockets in shadow | interrogation, menace, sports halls |
| Backlight / silhouette | shape without detail | mystery, reveals, horror thresholds |
| Single-source darkness | one small source, everything else black | horror, suspense |
| Neon / coloured practicals | saturated pools, wet reflections | night city, music video |
| Firelight / candle | warm, flickering, very low | intimacy, period, ritual |
| Studio softbox at 45° + gentle fill | even, texture-revealing, no hard shadows | portraits and character sheets [Tested] |

## Lighting for reference images [Tested]

- **Portraits and character sheets**: soft even studio lighting from a large softbox key at 45 degrees with gentle fill, no hard shadows, no rim light, neutral white balance, against a plain mid-grey seamless backdrop.
- **Real-photo identity sheets**: soft, texture-revealing side-angle lighting on pure white seamless.
- **Location plates**: name the actual source, plus the colour disagreement if there is one. The plate's light becomes the video's light, so get it right here.

## Lighting in video blocks

- Keep the light consistent with the plate: "Match @Image3's window daylight and contrast exactly" [Tested pattern].
- Let the subject move through light: walking into a pool of window light exposes the face for the key line [Tested pattern].
- Motivate any change: a door opening, a lamp switched on, a cloud passing. Write the cause.

```text
WEAK    The lighting becomes more intense as tension builds.
STRONG  At 8 s the hallway bulb flickers twice and dies; only the phone screen lights her face from below.
WHY     A physical event changes the light; "intensity" is a mood.
```

## Lighting by material (product work)

- **Metal and car paint**: large soft sources make long clean highlights; dark flags shape the reflections.
- **Glass and liquids**: backlight or edge light so the liquid glows; a rim separates it from the background.
- **Matte surfaces**: a soft key at 45° and gentle fill, keeping the texture visible.
- **Skin**: soft key, small speculars, real pores visible. Never "flawless".

## Realism tells

- Real rooms are a little under-lit and uneven. "The room is under-lit and a little too dim" [Tested phrasing for phone realism].
- Highlights clip near windows in phone footage [Tested]. Cinema cameras roll off gently instead.
- Name what the light falls *on*: dust in the beam, steam, rain, chalk dust hanging in the light.

## Lighting checks

- [ ] Every light is a named physical source, with its position, colour and quality.
- [ ] Video light matches the plate, and any change has a cause.
- [ ] The setup fits the genre and the playbook (practical and imperfect for UGC; controlled for product).
- [ ] No "good", "cinematic" or "dramatic lighting" anywhere.
