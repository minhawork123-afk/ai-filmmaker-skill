# Craft — Story Structure

How to shape seconds of AI video into something with a beginning, a turn and an ending. Unlabelled content is **[General best practice]**; workflow-proven rules are marked **[Tested]**.

## The unit is the beat

A beat is a moment where something changes for the viewer: new information, a new feeling, a decision, a reveal. Shots serve beats; blocks contain beats.

- One beat = one to three shots.
- One video block (12–20 s, 30 s max) = 2–5 beats [Tested block length].
- Split blocks **at** beats, never mid-moment [Tested].

```text
WEAK    Block 1 ends as she reaches for the door handle; Block 2 opens the door.
STRONG  Block 1 ends after the door swings open on darkness. Block 2 starts with her first step in.
WHY     A block boundary mid-action breaks motion continuity; a finished beat hands off cleanly.
```

## Three rules for short-form [Tested]

1. **Establish the world and the tension in the first 6 seconds.** No slow warm-up.
2. **Build through action, not dialogue.**
3. **End on an image, not an explanation.**

Persona and review content uses its own spine [Tested]: skeptical opener → one specific detail → one honest caveat → considered verdict.

## Hooks (first 1–3 seconds)

| Hook type | What the camera sees | Works for |
|---|---|---|
| In-motion open | Action already happening, mid-gesture | Action, product, UGC |
| Wrong detail | One thing out of place (a door open that should be shut) | Horror, thriller, mystery |
| Direct address | A face at the lens, saying a line with tension | UGC, testimonial |
| Extreme scale | A macro texture or an extreme wide | Product, nature, sci-fi |
| Sound-first | A specific sound before the image resolves | Horror, music video |

```text
WEAK    Opens on a slow pan across the city at sunset to set the mood.
STRONG  Opens mid-sprint: boots hit wet concrete, camera low and tracking, breath loud.
WHY     Motion and stakes in frame one; the world comes along for free.
```

## Beat maps by length

**6 s (one shot or one block):** hook image → turn. A single idea.

**15 s (one block):**
| 0–4 s | 4–8 s | 8–12 s | 12–15 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook / world + tension | Development | Turn / reveal | Image that lands |

**30 s (two blocks of ~15 s):** Block 1 = hook, setup, complication, ending on a turn. Block 2 = escalation, climax, final image.

**60 s (3–4 blocks):** world → disturbance → attempt → reversal → climax → final image. Each block owns one movement of the story and ends on a beat change.

**Teaser / trailer:** world → disturbance → escalation montage → title beat (edit) → stinger.

## Endings

- **Image ending:** the final shot holds long enough to land, usually 3–5 s.
- **Cut-on-the-gasp:** a hard cut at the peak (horror, UGC "stop recording").
- **Echo:** the final image mirrors the first with one thing changed.
- **Withheld reveal:** cut one beat before the full reveal (teasers).

```text
WEAK    She smiles and says, "And that's how I finally found peace."
STRONG  She sets the letter on the fire. It catches. She doesn't watch it burn.
WHY     The image says it; the explanation would undercut it.
```

## Writing the script for video

- Physical actions in [brackets] inline with dialogue, so each line maps onto a video segment [Tested].
- Speaking segments under 8 seconds each [Tested].
- Never write interior states; write behaviour [Tested].
- Dialogue does less work than you think. Prefer a look, an object or an action that carries the information.
- Give each character one concrete physical habit you can reuse across blocks (a ring they twist, a limp on the LEFT leg). It becomes continuity and character at the same time.

## Output format — beat sheet

```
BEAT SHEET — [title] · [total] s · [N] blocks
#  | Time    | Beat            | What the viewer sees                 | Line / key sound
1  | 0–4 s   | Hook            |                                      |
2  | 4–8 s   |                 |                                      |
...
Block split: Block 1 = beats 1–3 (0–15 s) · Block 2 = beats 4–6 (15–30 s)
Ending image: [the exact last frame]
```

## Story checks before the script is handed over

- [ ] The tension is visible by 6 s.
- [ ] Every beat changes something; delete the beats that only repeat.
- [ ] The ending is an image (or a hard cut) with time to land.
- [ ] Block splits fall on beat changes.
